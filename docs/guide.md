## The 3-day upload cycle

Do not upload multiple long videos in one day.

| Day | What to do |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | Choose one source story. Research its real English title and strongest twist. Make a simple story outline. |
| Day 2 | Write script, record narration, edit. Make thumbnail and title. |
| Day 3 | Upload one video. Reply to comments, pin one question, and record analytics after 48 hours. |
| Day 4 | Start the next story. Repeat the cycle. |

That gives you **2–3 quality videos per week**, not 15 rushed videos.

## What to upload

Choose stories with a clear, easy-to-understand hook:

- Reborn and takes revenge on ex/family
- Fake heiress becomes loved after family learns the truth
- Poor/ignored protagonist discovers hidden rich or powerful identity
- Doctor/surgeon reincarnated into a historical world
- Forced marriage turns into revenge or romance
- Betrayed husband/wife gets a second chance

Do not upload a random story just because you have footage. Ask:

1. Can I explain the story’s twist in one sentence?
2. Does it have a real source name people may search?
3. Is the first scene dramatic enough?
4. Can I make a complete, satisfying story—not unnecessary Part 1/Part 2?

Prefer complete stories. Avoid titles like only “Part 1” or “Season 2”; new viewers do not know why they should click.

## Title formula

Use the real story name plus one strong promise:

`[Actual Story Name] | She Was Reborn and Refused the Family That Ruined Her — Full Manhua Recap`

`[Actual Story Name] | A Genius Doctor Reborn in 1800s London — Full Recap`

Avoid very long titles, too many emojis, and generic phrases without the source name. Put the real source name in the first two lines of the description too.

## Thumbnail formula

Use:

- One main character, or two characters facing each other
- One visible emotion: shocked, betrayed, angry, crying
- Only 2–4 big words: `REBORN`, `FAKE HEIRESS`, `HE REGRETS IT`
- High contrast: dark background + bright face/text

Avoid:

- Many tiny comic panels
- Tiny speech bubbles
- Too much text
- A thumbnail that looks different from the title’s promise

## Script formula

Your first 30 seconds matter most.

- **0:00–0:15:** Main character + problem + twist  
  “After being reborn, she refuses the man who destroyed her life—but he starts remembering everything.”
- **0:15–0:45:** Explain who they are and what is at stake.
- **0:45 onward:** Start the story immediately.
- Every 30–60 seconds: add a new conflict, reveal, decision, or cliffhanger.
- Do not start with “Welcome back guys,” a long intro, or confusing dialogue with no context.
- Put “subscribe” only after the story has become interesting, not in the first few seconds.

| Metric | What it tells you | What to do |

| Impressions | How many people YouTube tested it with | Low impressions means YouTube is unsure of the audience/topic. |
| CTR | Thumbnail/title quality | Below 4%: improve title and thumbnail. 5–8%+: packaging is okay. |
| Average percentage viewed | Whether people stay | Below 10%: improve hook, narration, and pacing. |
| Traffic source | How viewers find you | Aim to slowly increase Browse and Suggested, not only Search. |
